Aurora - Discord Bot 

---

Features

- **Twitch Live Notiofications** - Make an announcement that you (or your friends) are live!
- **Fishing** - Fishing simulator with rare finds, trash, & treasure!
- **Magic 8 Ball** - Ask Aurora yes or no questions! (Ask too many , though and she might get sassy!)
- **Music** - Play a song from Youtube in a discord voice channel you're in - add songs to your queue and skip!
- **AI Conversations** - Uses OpenAI to generate responses.
	- **Typing Indicator** - Simulates natural response timing.
	- **Customizable Activities** - Will show your bot as "listening to, playing, etc.
	- **Personality** - Allows for dynamic AI conversation. 
- **Interactions** - GIF Interactions like "hug, kiss, slap". 
- **Memes** - Use commands to retrieve random memes. 
- **Starboard** - Let members vote (STAR) messages to be memorialized in your starboard!

---

Command		Command	Description
/8ball		Ask Aurora a yes/no question, get a random response
/fish		Play the interactive fishing simulator
/leaderboard	See who the real fishers are
/setup		Creates your role assignments in the channel you chose 
/join		Aurora joins a voice chat you are in 
/play		Plays a song with a search keyword. Add multiple songs to a queue
/skip 		Skips to next song in que 
/leave		Aurora will leave your voice channel 
/interaction	Send an interaction to other members of your discord! (i.e. bonk, poke, pat, highfive) 
/memes		Posts a random or wholesome meme
